# Privacy Policy — HatoControl

**Last updated:** July 7, 2026

HatoControl ("the app") is operated by Software Gamboa ("we", "us", "our"),
based in Costa Rica. This policy explains what information the app collects,
why, and how it's handled.

## Information we collect

- **Account email address** — used to create and authenticate your account,
  and to contact you about your account or subscription.
- **Photos** — if you choose to add a photo to a finca (farm), it's stored
  so it can display in the app and sync across your devices.
- **Farm management data you enter** — fincas, lotes (herd groups), animals
  and their identifiers, weights, diets, health/treatment records, purchases
  and sales, and related costs. This data is created by you to operate the
  app's core function (livestock record-keeping) and is linked to your
  account.

We do not collect this information for advertising or tracking purposes, and
we do not sell your data to third parties.

## How we use this information

- To provide the app's core functionality: offline-first record-keeping for
  your farm, synchronized across your devices.
- To authenticate you and enforce account/subscription limits.
- To provide customer support when you contact us.

## Where data is stored

Data is stored locally on your device and synchronized to our backend
(hosted on Supabase, a third-party infrastructure provider) over encrypted
connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your farm's data is restricted to
accounts you've explicitly added as members of that farm.

## Data sharing

We do not share your data with third parties except:
- Our infrastructure provider (Supabase), which stores and processes data
  on our behalf under its own security practices.
- When required by law.

## Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and
associated data by contacting us at the email below. Deleting your account
removes your access; some records may be retained as needed for legal or
accounting purposes.

## Children's privacy

HatoControl is a business tool for farm management and is not directed at,
or knowingly used by, children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be
reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
